TFG Preparation Manual

A structured guide for fourth-year Physics students, from the first idea to the last question of the defence

ManualFive partsPhysicsUPV/EHU

The manual follows the order in which the work actually happens: develop the project, write the report, present and defend it, with the tools in between and a checklist for the final weeks. Read Part I before you commit to a proposal and keep Part V at hand at the end.

Why this manual exists

STRUCTURE

A roadmap for the year

Each phase has a place and an order, which removes most of the uncertainty about what to do next and when.

SKILLS

Tools you will keep using

Version control, reproducible documents, citation discipline and public speaking serve well beyond the thesis.

STANDARD

What a good thesis looks like

The manual states what the committee expects, so the finished work demonstrates your growth rather than your guesswork.

Getting started

Begin with Part I to shape the project and set its foundations. Start early, keep a steady pace, and talk to your supervisor regularly: most problems are small when they are caught in time.
