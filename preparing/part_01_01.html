Part I – Development of the TFG Project

Comprehensive Manual for Trabajo de Fin de Grado (TFG) in Physics

Table of Contents

Part I – Development of the TFG Project

1.1 Understanding the Project

The objective of your Trabajo de Fin de Grado (TFG) is to demonstrate your ability to apply knowledge, analytical capabilities, and methodological skills acquired throughout your Physics studies. Choosing a topic aligned with your academic interests and career goals is essential. Reflect carefully on your personal strengths and the topics that genuinely excite you. Consider discussing preliminary ideas with faculty members or potential supervisors to refine your choice. Clearly define your research question and objectives to guide your project’s direction and scope.

1.2 Theoretical Preparation

Begin with an exhaustive literature review to establish a solid theoretical framework. This involves identifying key concepts, prior studies, and gaps your research will address. Utilize academic databases such as Google Scholar, JSTOR, and Scopus for comprehensive searches. Manage your literature systematically using reference tools such as Zotero, Mendeley, or BibTeX to facilitate easy citation management and bibliography creation. Summarize and critically evaluate relevant studies, noting significant findings, methodologies used, and limitations identified. This preparation ensures your research is informed by current scientific understanding and contributes meaningfully to existing knowledge.

1.3 Methodological Planning

Clearly outline your methodological approach, detailing whether it will involve experimental setups, computational modeling, theoretical calculations, or a combination. Develop a realistic and structured project timeline, setting clear milestones for each stage (literature review, experiments/simulations, data analysis, writing phases). Consistently maintain effective communication with your supervisor through regular meetings or updates, ensuring alignment and timely guidance. Anticipate potential obstacles and incorporate contingency plans into your timeline to accommodate unforeseen challenges.

1.4 Practical Work

Dedicate sufficient time to practical tasks, including reading scientific papers, writing initial drafts, conducting simulations (e.g., Python or R scripts for data analysis), laboratory experiments, or field observations. Document all activities meticulously, noting dates, methods, and results clearly. Regularly update a research journal or digital log to track your progress, problems encountered, and solutions implemented. This documentation will significantly streamline report writing and analysis. Regularly review your results with your supervisor to ensure accuracy and reliability. Ensure proper handling and storage of data, maintaining organized digital backups to prevent data loss.

1.5 Ethical Considerations

Ensure compliance with all ethical standards relevant to your research, particularly if it involves experimental subjects, sensitive data, or environmental considerations. Familiarize yourself with institutional ethics guidelines, obtaining necessary approvals and informed consents when applicable. Document clearly how ethical considerations have influenced your research design and practices.

Part II – Preparation of the Deliverable (Report)

2.1 Structure of the TFG Report

The TFG report must follow a clear and logical structure: - Introduction: Present your research topic, motivation, objectives, and research questions. - Theoretical Framework: Discuss existing theories, models, and previous research relevant to your topic. - Methodology: Describe the methods and approaches used to carry out your research. - Results: Clearly present your findings with appropriate figures, tables, and graphs. - Discussion and Analysis: Interpret your results, comparing them to previous studies and addressing research questions. - Conclusion: Summarize key findings, limitations, and suggest avenues for future research. - Bibliography: List all cited sources consistently using IEEE citation standards. - Appendices: Include supplementary material, raw data, code scripts, or detailed calculations.

2.2 Academic Writing Best Practices

Aim for clarity, precision, and coherence in your writing. Structure your arguments logically, avoiding ambiguity. Follow IEEE citation guidelines consistently to acknowledge sources and avoid plagiarism. Use clear, technical language, minimizing jargon unless necessary. Regularly proofread your drafts and seek feedback from peers or your supervisor to enhance readability and rigor.

2.3 Writing with an IDE

Use Visual Studio Code (VS Code) for an integrated writing experience combining code and text: - Installation: Download and install VS Code from the official website. - Essential Extensions: Markdown All-in-One, LaTeX Workshop, GitLens for enhanced productivity. - Alternatives: RStudio, Trae, Positron, each offering specific strengths depending on your coding and writing needs.

2.4 Quarto Ecosystem

Quarto is a powerful tool for academic writing: - Markdown Integration: Write comprehensive reports combining narrative and code. - Embedding Code: Insert code chunks (Python, R, Julia) directly into your documents. - Export Formats: Generate outputs in PDF, HTML, and interactive documents effortlessly.

2.5 Version Control

Understand and use Git effectively: - Git Basics: Create repositories, manage commits, branches, and merges. - Collaboration and Backup: Use GitHub to collaborate with peers and maintain version backups. - Integration with VS Code: Utilize built-in tools and extensions to streamline your workflow.

2.6 Environments and Execution

Manage computational environments to ensure reproducibility: - Virtual Environments: Create isolated Python environments (venv), R environments (renv), or Conda environments (conda). - Cross-Platform Reproducibility: Confirm consistent execution across Windows, macOS, and Linux. - Dependency Management: Clearly document and manage dependencies (requirements.txt, environment.yml).

This comprehensive guide ensures clarity and thoroughness in preparing for your TFG. Let me know if you’re ready to proceed with Part III, or if there are any further details you would like to include.
